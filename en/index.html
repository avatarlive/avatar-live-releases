Frequently asked questions

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes, both the monthly and yearly plans are commitment-free. You can cancel from the app (subscription management) at any time; access stays active until the end of the period you already paid for.

How many devices can I use my account on?

An account can be signed in on 2 devices at a time. Beyond that, you need to sign out an existing device before adding a new one.

Is my webcam footage sent over the internet?

No. Face and body tracking runs entirely locally on your machine — no camera footage is ever transmitted, unless you yourself start a recording or a stream.

Can I use my own VRM model?

Your avatar is provided and attached to your account after you subscribe. For a custom model, contact us after signing up.

Does Avatar Live work on Mac or Linux?

Not at the moment — only Windows 10/11 is supported today.
